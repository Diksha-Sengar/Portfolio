🌸 About Me

I'm a PM who loves building.

I'm a Product Manager with a CS engineering background — which means I can sit in a strategy meeting in the morning and debug an API in the afternoon. I genuinely love both.

Over the past few years, I’ve worked on AI-driven sales systems, CRM platforms, internal analytics tools, and automation workflows - designing products that help teams make smarter decisions and move faster (without losing their sanity in spreadsheets).
